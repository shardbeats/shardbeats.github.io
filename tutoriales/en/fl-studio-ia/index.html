FL Studio with AI: chord progressions with ChatGPT

If you search for FL Studio AI or AI in FL Studio, you are where you belong: in 5 steps you will generate a progression with ChatGPT and play it in the Piano Roll.

This FL Studio with AI guide is for beatmakers who already open FL Studio but get stuck on music theory. The trick known as AI in FL Studio does not make the beat for you: it gives you a harmonic starting point so you can add your ear, your drums and your arrangement. It also prepares you for understanding the parts of a beat (intro, verse, hook and outro) when arranging.

Step 1: define the idea, genre and BPM in FL Studio

Open a new project and decide two things before asking the AI anything: the genre and the tempo. For boombap try 88–95 BPM; for trap, 130–150 BPM. Set the BPM in the top bar of FL Studio and create an empty 4-bar pattern: with a clear tempo, the progression will fit your drums.

Step 2: ask ChatGPT for the progression

Open ChatGPT and ask for a progression in a specific key, with genre and BPM. The more specific you are, the better. Copy and adapt this example:

"Give me a chord progression in D minor for a boombap beat at 90 BPM, with simple 3-note voicings for piano"

Also ask for a hook variation, slightly more open, for contrast between sections without studying theory.

Step 3: play the chords in the Piano Roll

In FL Studio, open the Channel Rack, load a piano and open the Piano Roll. Draw each chord note by note, one chord per bar, following the ChatGPT notes. Start with long notes filling the bar and check by ear that each chord flows into the next.

Step 4: learn to quantize and add swing to humanize

If you played the chords with your MIDI keyboard, select the notes and learn to quantize (Alt+Q or the Piano Roll magnet) to fix the timing. Then apply some swing, nudge a few notes off the grid and vary each note's velocity. That controlled messiness makes the chords sound played by a person, not a machine.

FAQ

Can I use AI in FL Studio for free?

Yes. The free ChatGPT plan is enough to ask for progressions, and FL Studio includes the rest: Piano Roll, quantize, swing and export. No paid plugins needed.

Is the ChatGPT progression royalty-free?

Basic progressions are not protected by copyright: you can use and monetize your beats. What must be original is the rest: melodies, drums, arrangement and mix.

How do I move the chords to the Piano Roll and quantize them?

Draw or play each chord in the Piano Roll, select the notes and use quantize (Alt+Q) to snap them. Then apply swing and adjust velocity to humanize.
