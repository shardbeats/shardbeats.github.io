How to upload beats to YouTube

If your beat is finished and you want to reach listeners and artists, here you will learn to upload it to YouTube fast without wrestling with heavy editors.

Uploading beats to YouTube is the most direct way to showcase your catalog and attract artists. Many producers already have the WAV and artwork but get stuck creating the video. This guide takes you from audio to published video in under twenty minutes with a free tool.

Step 1: export your beat as WAV or stems

In your DAW, export the beat as a stereo WAV at 44.1 kHz and 16 or 24 bits, the format YouTube processes best. Leave headroom and avoid hard limiting. If you will send the track to artists, also save grouped stems —drums, bass and melodies— in a separate folder.

Step 2: prepare square artwork

Pick a square cover of at least 1080x1080 pixels, ideally 3000x3000. Use a sharp image with good contrast and no tiny text. Leave margin at the edges and export as JPG or PNG: it will be the only image in the video.

Step 3: create a 1080p MP4 video with TRAKKOUT

Turn audio and image into video without an editor. Open TRAKKOUT, free in TRAKK SUITE: upload your audio and artwork and generate a 1080p MP4 with the still image for the full beat. No timeline or manual rendering — you get a video ready to upload.

Step 4: upload the video to YouTube and fill in metadata

Go to YouTube Studio and drag in your MP4. Write a clear title with type and BPM —e.g., "Trap Beat 140 BPM – Night in La Piedad"— and add mood, BPM and contact links in the description. Add relevant tags, pick a sharp thumbnail and mark if it is for a general audience. Clean metadata helps discovery.

Step 5: publish and keep going with TRAKK SUITE

Check the preview, add an end screen with another beat or your playlist and publish. Share the link and reply to early comments. Then explore the rest of the free TRAKK SUITE tools to name beats and generate contracts.

FAQ

What format do I upload to YouTube for beats?

Upload an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. If you generate the video with TRAKKOUT at 1080p, it already meets the standard and YouTube processes it without aggressive recompression.

How do I make the video without an editor?

With TRAKKOUT you do not need Premiere or DaVinci. Just upload your WAV and square artwork: the tool automatically creates a 1080p MP4 with the still image and full audio.

Can it be 1080p with a single image?

Yes. YouTube accepts videos with a single static image for the whole duration. When you export at 1080p with TRAKKOUT, the video looks sharp on any screen and stays light for fast uploading.
