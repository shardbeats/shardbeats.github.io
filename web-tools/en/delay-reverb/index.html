How to use these times

Engineers often tune reverb to the tempo so it breathes with the track: pick a short pre-delay (1/64 or 1/32) and a long total (one or two bars); decay is the difference. For delay, try straight figures first for unnoticeable depth, and panned dotted or triplet figures for movement.

  • Lead snare: set the reverb to die just before the next hit.
  • Cleanup: cut lows (~600 Hz) and highs on the reverb send.
  • Depth: long reverbs on strings/pads, short and upfront on kick and bass.
How do you calculate delay time from BPM?

One beat is 60000 ÷ BPM in milliseconds. A whole note is 4 beats, half is 2, quarter is 1, eighth is 0.5, and so on. Dotted ×1.5, triplet ×⅔.

What pre-delay and decay should I use for reverb?

Short pre-delay (1/64 or 1/32) and a long total (1–2 bars). Decay = total − pre-delay. The preset table computes it for you.

What are the Hz values for?

To sync LFOs, tremolos and modulations to your song tempo.
