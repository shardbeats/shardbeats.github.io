What I collect

Only what you type in the form: your email address and, if you fill it in, your name. No card or payment details are requested.

What I use it for

  • Sending you the beat pack you asked for.
  • Sending you the new packs I release every week.
  • Nothing else. I do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.

Legal basis

Your explicit consent, given by ticking the checkbox in the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who else is involved

Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.) runs the mailing list and sends the emails. Your data is stored on their servers, which may be outside your country (including the United States), under their own contractual safeguards. See the Intuit privacy statement.

Google Analytics 4 measures visits and signups in aggregate with IP anonymization. GitHub Pages hosts the site. Google Drive hosts the pack you download. YouTube serves the embedded video, loaded from youtube-nocookie.com to limit tracking.

Cookies and consent

Measurement cookies are off by default through Google Consent Mode v2, and Mailchimp's tracking script is not loaded until you accept. When you arrive you will see a notice where you can accept or decline; your choice is kept in your browser under the sb-consent key and is never sent to a server. You can change it with the Cookies link in the footer.

How long it is kept

Your email stays on the Mailchimp list until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Analytics data is retained according to the retention setting on the Google Analytics property.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. For anything else, reach me on GitHub.
