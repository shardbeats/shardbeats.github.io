FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the beat pack really free?

Yes. The full pack is free in exchange for your email. No card and no payment details are requested.

Can I monetize the beats on Spotify and YouTube?

Yes. The license allows personal and commercial use, so you can monetize on Spotify, YouTube, TikTok and other platforms. The only restriction is reselling the beats as-is, unmodified.

How often do you release new packs?

Every week. When a new pack drops you get an email, and subscribers get access to every pack published so far.

Can I use the stems?

Yes. Every beat ships with 25 separate stems (kick, snare, hats, bass, melody and FX), so you have full control over the mix.

Do I have to credit you?

It is not required, but a credit like «Prod. by Shard Btmkr» is appreciated. And if you want, send me the finished song so I can listen to it.
