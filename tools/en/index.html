Guide

How to prep your library, step by step

The full workflow from a messy sample folder to a beat ready to publish.

1. Convert samples to the SP-404MK2 format

The Roland SP-404MK2 does not accept just any file. Use TRAKKCONVERTER and drop your whole folder in: it batch-converts to the right WAV and preserves your folder structure, so every sample stays where you expect it. If you'd rather do everything from one window, TRAKKSTUDIO bundles the same converter with organizing and normalization.

2. Normalize your peaks before loading

A sample at −18 dB next to one at −3 dB wrecks the balance of a beat. TRAKKNORMALIZER matches each clip to its own peak instead of pushing everything up by the same amount like a conventional normalizer — every kick lands even without clipping the loud ones. It processes thousands of files in batch with FFmpeg under the hood.

3. Name and protect the beat

Once the beat is done, TRAKKNAME generates titles by genre, mood and language so you stop burning time on names. To publish on YouTube, TRAKKOUT builds the 1080p MP4 from your audio and artwork. And if you're selling it or sending it to an artist, TRAKKLICENSE produces the license contract as a PDF in English or Spanish, without leaving your machine.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the TRAKK SUITE tools free?

Yes. All six tools are free and open source under the MIT license. No accounts, no subscriptions, no usage limits. Download them straight from GitHub Releases.

Do they work offline?

Yes. All processing happens on your own computer: conversion, normalization, naming, video and licensing. You only need internet for the initial download of the executable.

How do I convert samples to WAV for the SP-404MK2?

Download TRAKKCONVERTER, drag your sample folder into the window and pick an output folder. It batch-converts to the format and sample rate the Roland SP-404MK2 accepts while keeping your folder structure intact.

Which operating systems are supported?

The desktop tools are portable executables for Windows 10 and 11, with no installation required. TRAKKNAME also ships an Android version.

Can I sell beats made with these tools?

Yes. The software claims no rights over your music. To sell or license each beat, TRAKKLICENSE generates the contract as a PDF with delivery evidence.
