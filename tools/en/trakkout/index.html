TRAKKOUT

Beat + artwork to 1080p video

Your beat as audio only does not get watched on YouTube. TRAKKOUT builds the video with FFmpeg from your audio and cover, and optionally uploads it to your channel with the metadata already filled in.

Download for Windows View the source

What it does

  • 1920x1080 from your cover Auto-fit with crop or letterbox, plus an optional blurred background.
  • Metadata templates Variables like title, BPM, key or purchase link fill title, description and tags by themselves.
  • Scheduled publishing Sends the date to YouTube, and the video stays private until the chosen day.
  • Local history Every upload is logged in a SQLite database with its video ID.

How to use it

  1. Pick audio and artwork Drag the beat and the image in, or use Browse.
  2. Fill the metadata Beat, artist, BPM, key and purchase link.
  3. Generate the video FFmpeg builds the MP4 and you can preview it before uploading.
  4. Upload or schedule With your own Google account, to the channel you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account on some service of yours?

No. Uploads use your own Google Cloud project and your account, and the video goes to your channel.

Can I use it just to generate the MP4?

Yes. You can build the video and never upload it; the Google part is optional.

Can publishing be scheduled?

Yes. YouTube requires those videos to start as private, and the app does that automatically.

Is there an upload limit?

Your own project's daily YouTube API quota.
