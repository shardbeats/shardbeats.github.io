TRAKKLICENSE

Beat license contracts as PDF

When you sell a beat you need a contract, not a paragraph you copied somewhere. TRAKKLICENSE fills full templates in English and Spanish and exports them to PDF with a folio and evidence.

Download for Windows View the source

What it does

  • Four ready tiers MP3, WAV, Trackout and Unlimited non-exclusive leases, plus the exclusive, each with its own template.
  • Complete templates 25 sections: ownership, grant, royalties, Content ID, sync, term and termination.
  • Folio and evidence Every export gets a sequential folio that is never reused, plus a SHA256 of the text and the template version.
  • Editable without breaking the original Edits go to copies; factory text can be restored whenever you want.

How to use it

  1. Pick the tier Non-exclusive lease or exclusive.
  2. Fill the sale Buyer, price, term, country and, if you have them, the IPIs.
  3. Check the preview The contract updates as you type.
  4. Export to PDF Saved with folio, hash and a history record.

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a self-help tool that fills templates with the values you type. For a significant deal, have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review the contract.

Can I edit the contract text?

Yes. Edits are stored on a copy, so the factory text stays available.

How do I prove a sale happened?

Every export carries a sequential folio and a SHA256 of the rendered text, stored in the local history.

Does anything go to the internet?

No. The app runs offline and ships no third-party binaries.
